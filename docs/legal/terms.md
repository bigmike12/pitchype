## 1. About these terms

1.1 Pitchype (“we”, “us”) runs a website and app where businesses and
creators find each other and agree paid work. We are `[legal entity name,

1.2 By creating an account or using Pitchype you agree to these terms and to

1.3 You must be at least `[18]` years old and able to enter a contract. If

## 2. What Pitchype is, and isn't

2.1 Pitchype is a marketplace. A **business** can post a **campaign** or
approach a **creator** directly. When a business hires a creator for a job,
that is an **engagement**. The contract for the work is between the business
and the creator. We are not a party to it, we don't employ creators, and we
are not their agent.

2.2 We provide tools: profiles, search, chat, payments held until work is
approved, and an admin team that helps when people disagree. We don't
guarantee that any creator will be hired, that any campaign will get results
(reach, sales, followers), or that anyone will behave well.

2.3 Creators are independent. They are responsible for their own taxes and
for the content they post.

## 3. Accounts

3.1 One account is one role: a business or a creator. If you are both, use two
accounts with different email addresses.

3.2 Give accurate information and keep it up to date. Keep your password
private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

3.3 Follower numbers are **self-reported ranges** unless marked “Verified”. A
Verified badge means an admin checked a code Pitchype issued against the
creator's profile on the date shown. It is not a guarantee of reach,
audience quality or identity.

3.4 We may refuse, suspend or close accounts that break these terms (see
section 10).

## 4. Campaigns and hiring

4.1 A campaign states the brief, deliverables, platforms, requirements,
number of places, price per creator, application deadline and deliver-by
date. Prices are in naira, whole naira, at least ₦5,000.

4.2 A business's first campaign is reviewed by us before it is listed. Later
campaigns are listed straight away. We may remove any campaign.

4.3 Once a campaign is live the business cannot change the price,
requirements, deliverables or destination link. It can change wording,
extend the deadline, add places and close the campaign early. A creator keeps
the terms they were approved under.

4.4 A creator may propose their own price when applying (at least ₦5,000). A
business approves the price it was shown. If the creator changes it before
approval, the approval is refused and the business sees the new price.

4.5 Approving an application creates an engagement waiting for payment. The
business must pay within **48 hours** or the approval lapses, the place is
freed and the creator may apply again. Nothing starts until the engagement is
paid.

4.6 Only a business messages first. Creators reply in conversations a
business started.

## 5. Payments, fees and escrow

Paystack. You also agree to Paystack's terms when you pay or are paid through
it. We never see or store a full card number.

5.2 **Business fee.** The business pays the agreed price plus a platform fee
of **5% of the price (minimum ₦500)**, added on top. For a ₦50,000 job the
business pays ₦52,500. The business sees the exact total before paying.

5.3 **Escrow.** When a business pays, the agreed price is held for that
engagement. Escrow is a record in Pitchype's books of whose money is held for
which job. `[Counsel: describe how and where the cash is held, once the

5.4 **Creator fee.** When money is released to a creator, we deduct **5% of

5.5 **Fees are set in our system** and recorded on each engagement when it is
paid. A later fee change does not affect an engagement already paid.

5.6 **Payouts.** A creator can ask to withdraw their wallet balance to their
own bank account, minimum ₦1,000. Before confirming, the creator sees the

is approved by us first, and we may refuse or return one with a written
reason (the amount goes back to the wallet). The bank name is checked
against the account number before it is saved. The destination is fixed when
the payout is requested.

5.7 **Business wallet.** A refund goes to the business's Pitchype wallet, not

## 6. Doing and reviewing the work

6.1 A creator must not start until the engagement is paid. Work is
“started” when the creator presses **Start work** on a paid engagement, after
a confirmation.

6.2 When finished, the creator submits proof: 1 to 10 links, up to 5
screenshots and a short note, with at least one link or one screenshot.

6.3 The business then has **7 days** to review. It can:
- **accept**, which releases escrow to the creator (less the creator fee); or
- **deny**, which needs one or more written reasons the creator can see. The
  creator can resubmit at most 3 times or ask us to resolve
  the matter.

6.4 **Automatic release.** If the business does not accept or deny within 7
days of the submission, escrow is released to the creator automatically. The
7 days stop if the business denies or a dispute is opened, and start again on
a resubmission. We send reminders before the deadline, but the release
happens whether or not you read them. `[Counsel: confirm this is
enforceable and fairly disclosed.]`

6.5 Both sides should keep the work, content and agreement on Pitchype so we
can help if there is a problem. We can't help with money or work agreed off
the platform.

## 7. Cancellations, refunds and disputes

7.1 **The business fee is non-refundable** once the business has paid, except
where 7.2(c) says otherwise. We tell the business this at checkout and again
on the cancel screen, and we record that they saw it.

7.2 What happens when an engagement ends early (amounts for a ₦50,000 job):

| Situation | Business gets back | Pitchype keeps |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Business cancels after paying, work **not** started | The agreed price (₦50,000) | The business fee (₦2,500) |
| (b) Business wants to cancel after work **started** | Only by the creator's agreement, or through a dispute. No cancelling alone | The business fee |
| (c) Creator withdraws or stops responding, or we find the creator at fault | The agreed price plus the business fee, less the card charge on that payment | Nothing beyond the card charge |
| (d) Dispute: we release in full to the creator | Nothing | Both fees (normal completion) |
| (e) Dispute: we refund in full to the business, creator not at fault | The agreed price | The business fee |
| (f) Dispute: split | The refunded share of the price | The business fee, plus the creator fee on the share released |

confirm]`.

7.4 **Disputes.** Either person can ask us to resolve a disagreement about an
