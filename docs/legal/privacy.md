## 2. What we collect

**You give us**

| What | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and password (password is handled by our sign-in provider; we can't read it) | Everyone | Create and secure your account, send account emails |
| Account type (business or creator) | Everyone | Show the right tools |
| Business name, contact name, state and city | Businesses | Run your account and campaigns |
| Display name, short bio, photo, state and city, niches, platforms with handles and self-reported follower ranges, optional starting rate | Creators | Build your public profile and the directory |
| Verification code and the follower count an admin saw | Creators who verify | Confirm a handle is yours |
| Campaign details: brief, deliverables, requirements, price, dates, destination link | Businesses | List the campaign |
| Applications, proposed prices | Creators | Apply for work |
| Chat messages | Everyone | Let you negotiate and agree work |
| Proof of work you submit `[when Stage 7 ships]` | Creators | Let the business review it |
| Bank name, account number, account holder name | Creators requesting payouts | Pay you. The full number goes to Paystack to check the name and set up the transfer. **We keep only the bank, last four digits, name and Paystack's recipient code.** |
| Reports, appeals and what you write in them | Anyone who reports or appeals | Keep the platform safe |

**We create**

- **Money records**: payments you make or receive, fees, wallet movements,
  payouts, and the Paystack reference for each. These form our ledger, which
  is never edited or deleted.
- **Notifications** we send you in the app.
- **Safety records**: strikes, blocks, suspensions, admin actions and the
  written reasons for them.
- **Technical data**: your IP address is used briefly in the server's memory
  to limit repeated attempts (sign-ups, messages, bank checks); it isn't saved
  with your profile. Our hosting and database providers keep ordinary server
  logs. `[Counsel/engineering: confirm log content and retention.]`

**Cookies.** We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. We don't
use advertising or analytics trackers. `[Re-check before launch.]`

## 3. What other people can see

- **Signed-out visitors** see a creator's display name, photo, niches, city and
  state, platforms and follower ranges. Profiles are visible only after the
  creator publishes them.
- **Signed-in businesses** also see handles, bio and starting rate.
- **Search engines** may list creator profiles unless the creator opts out in
  their settings.
- **Campaigns** are visible only to signed-in creators, admins and the
  business that posted them.
- **A creator you apply to or a business you hire** sees what they need to
  work with you (names, messages, the price, the proof you submit).
- **Admins** can see what's needed to review reports, verifications, money
  and disputes.

We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to **see**, **correct**, **delete** or **export** your data, to
**object** to or **restrict** some uses, and to **withdraw consent** where we
rely on it. Email `hello@pitchype.com`. We may need to confirm it's you, and
some money and safety records can't be erased (see section 6). You can also
complain to `[the Nigeria Data Protection Commission]`.

## 8. Security

Access to data is limited by role and by database rules, so people can see
only their own records and what the product shows them. Admin tools are
restricted. The secret key for our database never reaches the browser, and
bank details beyond the last four digits are never stored. No system is
perfectly secure; tell us at `hello@pitchype.com` if you see a problem.
`[Breach notification process: counsel/engineering.]`

## 9. Children

Pitchype is for people `[18]` and over. We don't knowingly collect data from
children. If you think we have, email us and we'll remove it.
